Seasonal Planning

Farming by the Seasons

Tamil Nadu has distinct cropping seasons. Planning your crops according to these seasons maximizes yield and minimizes climate risks.

Kharif (Kuruvai / Sornavari)

June to September. Ideal for Paddy, Millets, Cotton, and Groundnut.

Rabi (Samba / Thaladi)

October to January. Major season for Paddy, Pulses, and Maize.

Summer (Navarai)

February to May. Suitable for Groundnut, Gingelly, and irrigated vegetables.

Soil Health

Soil & Fertilizer Guidance

Healthy soil is the foundation of a good harvest. Learn how to maintain and improve your soil quality.

Soil Testing

Test your soil before every major season to understand nutrient deficiencies and pH levels.

Organic Manure

Apply Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or compost at 12.5 t/ha to improve soil structure and water retention.

Nutrient Management

Apply NPK fertilizers as per soil test recommendations. Use split doses for Nitrogen to reduce leaching.

Water Management

Smart Irrigation Guidance

Efficient water management is crucial in Tamil Nadu. Adopting modern irrigation techniques can save up to 40% water.

  • Drip Irrigation

    Highly recommended for Sugarcane, Banana, and Cotton. Delivers water directly to roots.

  • Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD)

    For Paddy fields, allow the soil to dry slightly before re-flooding to save water without yield loss.
