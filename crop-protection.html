Pest Identification

Common Crop Pests

Yellow Stem Borer

Affected: Paddy

Symptoms: 'Dead heart' in vegetative stage and 'white ear' in reproductive stage.

Aphids

Affected: Cotton, Vegetables

Symptoms: Curling of leaves, stunted growth, sooty mold on honeydew.

Fall Armyworm

Affected: Maize

Symptoms: Ragged holes in leaves, excessive frass inside the whorl.

Eco-Friendly

Natural & Organic Protection

Reduce chemical dependency by integrating natural pest control methods into your farming practices.

  • Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) - Highly effective botanical insecticide for sucking pests.
  • Pheromone Traps - Install 12/ha to monitor and trap adult male moths (e.g., for Stem Borer).
  • Crop Rotation - Break the life cycle of soil-borne pathogens by rotating with non-host crops like pulses.

Need Expert Help?

If you notice severe crop damage, do not rely solely on general advice. Consult a local agricultural officer before applying chemical pesticides.

Biological Control

Friends of the Farmer

Not all insects are pests. Learn to identify and encourage beneficial insects and microbes that naturally protect your crops.

Ladybird Beetles

Natural predators that aggressively feed on aphids, mites, and scale insects, keeping their populations in check.

Trichoderma Viride

A powerful bio-fungicide used for seed treatment and soil application to prevent dangerous root rots.

Trichogramma

Tiny parasitic wasps that destroy the eggs of destructive stem borers and bollworms before they hatch.

Don't let weeds steal your yield

Weeds fiercely compete for nutrients, water, and sunlight, potentially reducing crop yields by up to 30% if left unchecked.

Crop Care

Integrated Weed Management

Effective weed control requires a combination of strategies tailored to your specific crop and soil.

Manual & Mechanical

Timely inter-cultivation and hand weeding at critical early growth stages.

Chemical Control

Calculated, safe use of pre-emergence and post-emergence herbicides.

Cultural Practices

Mulching and maintaining proper plant spacing to suppress weed growth.

Root Level

Soil-Borne Disease Prevention

Healthy roots equal a healthy harvest. Protect your crops from the ground up.

Solarization

Cover moist soil with transparent polythene sheets during summer for 4-6 weeks to naturally kill pathogens and weed seeds using intense solar heat.

Seed Treatment

Always treat seeds with recommended fungicides or bio-agents before sowing to aggressively prevent early damping-off and sudden root rot.

Proper Drainage

Waterlogging severely encourages fungal wilt diseases. Ensure fields are leveled and have proper channels to drain excess rainwater quickly.

Safety First

Safe Handling of Agrochemicals

Protect yourself, your family, and the environment when applying chemical pesticides and fertilizers.

Wear Protection

Always use robust gloves, masks, and full-sleeved clothing while mixing and spraying chemicals.

Check Wind

Never spray against the wind direction to completely avoid dangerous inhalation and chemical drift.

Exact Dosage

Follow the recommended dosage strictly. Overuse heavily leads to permanent pest resistance.

Safe Disposal

Triple-rinse empty containers and puncture them. Never wash equipment in local ponds.
