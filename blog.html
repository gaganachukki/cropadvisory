Smart Market Insights

Harvest Timing

Tomato prices are historically highest during November-December in Tamil Nadu. Plan your transplanting accordingly to catch the high-price window.

Value Addition

Instead of selling raw turmeric, curing and polishing it can fetch 20-30% higher prices in Erode and Salem mandis.

Reducing Losses

Use proper plastic crates for transporting vegetables like tomatoes and bananas to reduce post-harvest physical damage and maintain premium quality.
