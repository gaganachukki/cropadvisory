About STACKLY

Empowering Farmers with Technology & Knowledge

Our Mission

Growing Better, Together

STACKLY was built to provide localized, timely, and scientific agricultural guidance to farmers across Tamil Nadu. We believe that by combining traditional farming wisdom with modern data-driven insights, we can significantly improve yield, profitability, and sustainability for local farmers.

Our platform acts as a bridge between agronomy experts, real-time weather and market data, and the farmers working hard in the fields.

  • Localized Crop Recommendations
  • Weather & Market Insights
  • Pest & Disease Early Warnings
Our Vision

"To be the digital backbone of every farm in Tamil Nadu, ensuring food security and prosperous farmers through sustainable, tech-enabled agriculture."

Our Story

The STACKLY Journey

From a small pilot project to Tamil Nadu's leading crop advisory platform.

2022

The Inception

Started as a research project with 50 local paddy farmers in Thanjavur.

2024

Platform Launch

Officially launched the STACKLY web platform covering 10 major commercial crops.

2026

Statewide Expansion

Reached 25,000+ active farmers across all 38 districts of Tamil Nadu.
